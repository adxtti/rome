# ROME — Rockstar Online Modding Engine (Unofficial)

> **Unofficial concept. Strictly experimental.**
> ROME is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. "Rockstar" and "Grand Theft Auto" are trademarks of their respective owners.

ROME is a community multiplayer and modding framework for the Grand Theft Auto VI PC port effort, in the spirit of FiveM.

## Goals

- **Multiplayer:** dedicated servers, client sync, and session management
- **Scripting:** a scripting runtime for server- and client-side resources
- **Resources:** a packaging and loading system for community mods

## Status

Early concept. Nothing here is playable yet.
